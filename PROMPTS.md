# Ready-made trials

Paste any of these into Pi after installing it. Fill in the brackets with as much detail
as you can: numbers, who it is for, what you have tried. The jury only knows what you tell it.

1. **A business idea:** `/skill:court I want to start [business] for [customer]. I have [money/time], [what I've done so far], and I'd charge [price].`
2. **Quitting your job:** `/skill:court Quit my job as [job] to do [plan] full time. I have [savings], [income so far], and my monthly costs are [costs].`
3. **A post before you publish it:** `/skill:court This post will get more engagement than my usual posts: [paste the post]. My usual posts get [numbers].`
4. **A big purchase:** `/skill:court Buy [thing] for [price]. I'd use it for [reason]. The alternative is [alternative].`
5. **Your code plan:** `/skill:court Rewrite [part of the app] in [new approach] instead of [current approach]. Here's why: [reasons].`
6. **A claim you believe:** `/skill:court [the claim]. Here's my evidence: [evidence].`
7. **A second opinion on the model's own answer:** `/skill:court The plan you just gave me is the right one.` (run it right after the agent suggests something)

Short on usage? Add `--quick` for a jury of 6.
